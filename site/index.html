FASTA Toolkit

Prepare protein FASTA files for search engines: clean up and merge, make target-decoy databases, check decoy quality, convert. Built on fastatacular, peptacular and pefftacular.

Private by design: everything runs in your browser (Python via Pyodide). Your files are never uploaded. To browse and search entries, use the FASTA Viewer.
